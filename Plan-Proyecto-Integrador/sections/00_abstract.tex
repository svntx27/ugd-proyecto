\section*{ABSTRACT}

The academic and administrative management of doctoral scholarship recipients at the Central University of Ecuador (UCE) faces critical challenges due to reliance on isolated spreadsheets. This practice causes data fragmentation, lack of automated expiration alerts, delays in certificate issuance, and operational disorder, forcing students to physically visit the office repeatedly just to ask which documents they have submitted or are missing.

To resolve this problem, this project proposes the design and implementation of a decoupled modular web platform: the Doctoral Management System (SGD), interconnected via an Express.js REST API with a normalized PostgreSQL database and Prisma ORM. The solution provides an Administrative Panel in Next.js 16 (React 19) with RBAC security for Unit staff to manage doctoral programs, student records, and an appointment calendar without spreadsheets. In parallel, it incorporates a fast Student Portal in Astro and React where doctoral scholars can review the status of their records with total transparency (identifying submitted vs. missing requirements), schedule real-time online appointments with Unit staff for personalized guidance, and manage requests, alerts, and digital certificates. The platform is evaluated through a cloud functional demo on a synthetic benchmark with fictional profiles based on official institutional formats under the LOPDP, supported by interactive documentation in Fumadocs.

\textbf{Keywords:} Doctoral Management System (SGD), Decoupled Architecture, Next.js 16, Astro, Prisma ORM, Role-Based Access Control, Real-Time Appointment Booking, Fumadocs, UCE.
